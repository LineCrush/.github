# Community code of conduct

LineCrush wants its public spaces to be useful, welcoming, and safe for everyone participating in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Discuss ideas and behavior, not a person's identity or worth.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent until the evidence says otherwise.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property.
- Follow applicable laws, platform rules, and responsible-gambling standards.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized content or unwanted sexual attention;
- doxxing, impersonation, or publishing private information;
- spam, scams, undisclosed promotion, or coordinated manipulation;
- encouraging unsafe gambling behavior or targeting minors; and
- any conduct that disrupts the community or puts users at risk.

## Enforcement

LineCrush maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.

Report conduct concerns privately through the [LineCrush contact page](https://www.linecrush.com/contact) or [hello@linecrush.com](mailto:hello@linecrush.com). Include links and context, but do not forward unnecessary personal information.

This code applies in LineCrush-managed GitHub repositories and other official community spaces whenever someone is representing LineCrush or participating in a LineCrush community.
