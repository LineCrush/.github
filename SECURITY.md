# Security policy

LineCrush takes reports about the security and privacy of our website, applications, APIs, and public repositories seriously.

## Report a vulnerability privately

Email [hello@linecrush.com](mailto:hello@linecrush.com) with the subject **Security report**. Please include:

- the affected URL, application, endpoint, or repository;
- a clear description of the issue and its potential impact;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- relevant timestamps, request IDs, screenshots, or logs with secrets and personal data removed; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not open a public GitHub issue, pull request, Discord thread, or social post for a suspected vulnerability. Do not include passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment details, private user data, or other secrets in your report.

## Research guidelines

Please act in good faith and avoid:

- accessing, changing, downloading, or retaining data that does not belong to you;
- degrading service, creating excessive traffic, or testing denial-of-service conditions;
- automated scanning that could affect production availability;
- social engineering, phishing, spam, or physical attacks; and
- publishing details before LineCrush has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate the issue.

Stop testing and report the issue if you encounter personal data, credentials, or a path that could affect other users.

## Scope and rewards

This policy covers public LineCrush-owned web properties, applications, APIs, and repositories. Third-party services, sportsbook platforms, and vendor systems are outside our authorization and should be reported to their owners.

LineCrush does not currently operate a public bug bounty program. A report does not create an entitlement to payment, but responsible reports are appreciated and will be reviewed.
